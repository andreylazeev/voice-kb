VoiceKB modifications: selected single Cyrillic words with unambiguous profanity stems,
lowercased and deduplicated both lists, excluded ambiguous spellings and slur compounds,
and filled in past-tense forms of common verbs. The merged entries feed both tap and
swipe vocabularies and use the existing opt-in for profanity suggestions.
